## 🎯 Release summary

Update platform validation to follow the legitimate first-run redirect from /login and accept either the normal login page or the administrator setup page. The curl call now uses --location --max-redirs 3 and the grep checks for either "Sign in" or "Set up your administrator account". Also update unit tests to assert the new curl flags and the combined regex are present in the validation script.
